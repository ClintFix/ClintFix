### Sage Teacher

An interactive AI teacher for kids ages 4–8. It adjusts reading, math, and other lessons as kids work through them, so they can practice what they need and move on when they’re ready. Parents can see what their child worked on and talk with Sage about what to focus on next. [Sage Teacher](https://www.sageteacher.ai/)

### Sage Library

An iPad library of illustrated fairy tales, fables, and classic books. Kids can read independently or listen while each word is highlighted. They can ask Sage what a word means or get help finding their next book, so they can keep reading when they get stuck. [Sage Library](https://sagelibrary.ai/)

### NRL Hunter

I built the platform that runs the NRL Hunter competition series. Competitors can find matches, register, and track their results and season standings. Match directors can manage registration, check-in, scoring, and results in one place. [NRL Hunter](https://www.nrlhunter.org/)

### Targetsmith

I built Targetsmith when I needed custom steel targets for a match. It lets match directors design target shapes, lay out a full sheet of steel, and export DXF files they can send to a fabrication shop. [Targetsmith](https://targetsmith.app/)

### Short Bus Precision Talking Timer

I designed and built this rechargeable talking countdown timer for NRL Hunter–style shooting competitions. It calls out the time remaining so competitors can keep track of the clock without looking at a display. [Short Bus Precision Talking Timer](https://shortbusprecision.com/products/short-bus-precision-talking-timer)
